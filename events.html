About the event

Coldplay bring the Music of the Spheres World Tour to NICE Grounds, Bengaluru — a stadium-scale night of wristband light shows, pyrotechnics, and singalongs built for tens of thousands of voices. Expect the hits spanning two decades, a few surprises, and the band's signature big-hearted spectacle carried live end to end.
